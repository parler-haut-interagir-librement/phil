# Transparency and privacy

A person is owed three things beside a model: to know they are talking to one, to know what it made, and to have
their data handled as the rest of the application handles it. The bundle gives the application the pieces; where to
show them is the application's.

## Notices

`AiNotice` reads the notices of the translation domain `phil_ai_notice`, shipped in English and French:

```php
use Phil\AiBundle\Application\AiNotice;
use Phil\AiBundle\Domain\Journal\Output;

$notice->interaction($locale);                  // "You are talking to an AI. Its answers are suggestions: …"
$notice->generated(Output::Image, $locale);     // "This image was generated by AI. Nothing is applied until you accept it."
```

`interaction` goes beside a conversation or an assistant, before the first exchange; `generated.text`,
`generated.image` and `generated.speech` beside what a suggestion shows. An application rewords them, or adds a
language, in its own `translations/phil_ai_notice.<locale>.*` file, which wins over the bundle's.

## Disclosure of what was made

`Suggestion::disclosure()` says how the work that takes the suggestion was made, as a `CreationDisclosure` of
`phil/creation-disclosure`: its part — text, image or audio — declared `trainedAlgorithmicMedia` when the suggestion
is used as proposed, or `compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia` when the person changed it:

```php
$tasks->acceptEdited($suggestion->id);
$chapter->disclose($suggestion->disclosure(edited: true));    // GenerativeAi::Partial
```

The application merges it into the disclosure of the whole work, which `phil/creation-disclosure` writes in XMP and
schema.org, and which a reader filters on. A work made without AI says so by the author's choice, never by the
bundle's.

## The usage register

`AiUsageRegister::uses()` lists the application's uses of AI, read from its tasks: each kind, its `purpose` from the
configuration, what it asks for, the platform and model, and the tools the model may propose. It is what a
transparency page, a privacy policy or an audit lists, and it stays true by being the configuration itself:

```yaml
phil_ai:
    tasks:
        draft.rewrite: { platform: main, model: 'the-model', purpose: 'Proposes a rewrite of a passage the author chose.' }
```

Every use proposes and a person decides: the register needs no column for it.

## Privacy requests

With `phil/privacy-bundle` installed, `Infrastructure\Privacy\AiJournalPrivacy` is its module `ai`, registered as an
exporter and an eraser: the data subject is the subject tasks are charged to. The export lists the subject's tasks —
kind, output, platform, model, whose key, outcome, tokens, cost, when, how long, decision — which is all the journal
holds; the erasure deletes the subject's rows, and a second erasure finds nothing left. A subject that names a
project rather than a person is erased with the project, by `AiTasks::erase()`.
