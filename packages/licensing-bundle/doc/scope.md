# Scope

What the bundle does, and what stays out of it.

## In the bundle

The catalog of licenses and what each requires, the declaration of a work and the attribution of each resource, the
compliance check and the export guard, the guard of the prints with its strict setting, the license and attribution
page, the attribution form, and the license finder with its custom terms.

## Not started

- **Community content programs**: a license offered to the creators of a platform's community, and the sharing of the
  revenue of what they sell under it. No part of them exists in the bundle.

## Out of the bundle

- What the checks cannot see — that the notices and the changes stated are true — stays the publisher's.
- The bundle ships no license text; the license, at its address, is what binds.
