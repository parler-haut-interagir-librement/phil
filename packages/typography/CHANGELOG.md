# Changelog

All notable changes to `phil/typography` will be documented in this file.

Updates should follow the [Keep a CHANGELOG](https://keepachangelog.com/) principles.

## 0.2.0-alpha - 2026-11-18

The next version is 0.2.0-alpha: a text longer than 1 MiB, fixed before, is refused by default.

### Added

- `Exception\InputTooLarge`, thrown by `Typographer::fix()`, `fixDocument()`, `fixText()` and `Typographers::fixHtml()`
  for a text longer than the ceiling, before it is parsed; `$bytes` and `$limit` say both lengths, and `check()`
  refuses an input above a ceiling.
- `maxBytes`, the ceiling of a text in bytes, on `Typographer`, `Typographer::for()` and `Typographers`, which gives it
  to each typographer it builds; `Typographer::DEFAULT_MAX_BYTES`, 1 MiB.
- The section [The ceiling of a text](doc/html.md#the-ceiling-of-a-text).

### Changed

- Every entry point refuses a text longer than 1 MiB unless configured.

## 0.1.1-alpha - 2026-11-17

The next version is 0.1.1-alpha: documentation only.

### Changed

- The description of `composer.json`, shown on Packagist, says what the package does.
- The README, in English and in French, says what the package does, without the wording of its first drafts.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` says what the package does and how to work on it, without the wording of its first drafts.
- `data/LICENSES` and the page on hyphenation describe the patterns written for this library without that wording.

## 0.1.0-alpha - 2026-11-11

### Added

- `Locale`, a language with an optional script and region, and its candidates from the most precise.
- `Run`, the text nodes of one block in parts, with locked parts for protected elements, and `Fixer`, the interface
  of one rule.
- The fixers `UnicodeNormalization`, `Ellipsis`, `Dimension`, `Dash` with `DashStyle`, `SmartQuotes`, `CurlyQuote`,
  `NoSpaceBeforeComma`, `SpaceBeforePunctuation` with `FrenchSpacing` for France and Canada, `Trademark`, `Unit`,
  `Hyphen` and `WordBreak`, the zero-width spaces between Thai words from the ICU dictionary; emoji sequences left byte
  for byte by every one. `SmartQuotes` opens a quote after `>`, the end of a tag escaped as text, and closes an inner
  one before `<`.
- `QuoteStyle` and `QuoteStyles`: the quotation marks of 66 languages and regions, after the Unicode CLDR.
- `Patterns`, `Hyphenator` and `Dictionary`: Liang's hyphenation algorithm, never before a combining mark, with the
  hyph-utf8 patterns of 69 languages whose licence permits them, their exceptions and minimum lengths, recorded in
  `data/LICENSES`, and patterns of its own under MIT for Czech, Hungarian, Armenian, Indonesian, Latvian, Macedonian and
  Romanian, generated by `bin/generate-patterns.php` from the orthographic rules of each language and tested on lists of
  common words; Armenian and Macedonian are experimental, served only by a `Dictionary` built with them
  (`Dictionary::EXPERIMENTAL`).
- `Typographer`: HTML fragments, whole documents and plain text fixed through `Dom\HTMLDocument`, text only, block by
  block; `pre`, `code`, `kbd`, `script`, `style` and `textarea` protected by default, the list configurable; `<` and
  `>` escaped in attribute values, as the current HTML standard serializes them.
- `Preset`, the fixers of each locale by name and in order, and `Typographers`, one typographer per locale, configured
  by fixer names, those of 64 locales kept at most; `Typographers::fixHtml()`, each passage whose `lang` names another
  language fixed by the typographer of that language.
- `TypographyException`, implemented by `InvalidLocale`, `UnsupportedLanguage` and `UnknownFixer`.
- The documentation in `doc/`, one file per topic, indexed by the README.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md` and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
